The problem

F&I paperwork gets worse every single year.

More disclosures. More state variations. More OEM requirements. Meanwhile the person who's about to hand you thirty‑eight thousand dollars is watching your F&I manager hunt for the right version of a form.

Every one of those minutes costs you something.

  • 01Training time. New F&I hires spend weeks learning which form goes with which deal, in which state.
  • 02Errors. A wrong box on a title application comes back to you weeks later, and it comes back to you.
  • 03Deal time. The longer someone sits in the office, the more time they have to reconsider.
  • 04Exposure. Customer SSNs and bank details living in a filing cabinet is a bet you don't want to be making.

How it works

One deal, start to jacket.

The order below is the actual order of a sale. DocuRide covers all of it.

01CRM

The customer walks in

Their information goes in once. Every document after this pulls from it, so nothing gets retyped and nothing gets mistyped.

02Credit

Credit application

Collected on a secure form, encrypted the moment it's entered. No paper application sitting on a desk with a social security number on it.

03Docs

Documents build themselves

Bill of sale, odometer statement, title application, OEM forms, and whatever specific document your state or county insists on. A few clicks, correct the first time.

04Sign

Signatures, all in one place

Compliant electronic signature on everything — including documents that came from somewhere other than DocuRide. Your customer signs once, not fourteen times.

05Jacket

The jacket goes to the cloud

The completed deal is stored, searchable, and audit‑logged. When someone asks for a deal from eighteen months ago, you find it in seconds instead of a basement.

Compliance

The boring part, handled.

Three acronyms decide whether a regulator's visit is a formality or a very bad week. DocuRide is built around them.

FACTA

Red Flags and disposal

Identity theft warning signs get caught during the deal, and customer data is disposed of properly instead of sitting in a drawer forever.

GLBA

Safeguards Rule

Financial information is encrypted in transit and at rest, with access controls and an audit trail on every record.

OFAC

Screening in the flow

Screening happens as part of the deal, not as a separate task somebody remembers to do later.
